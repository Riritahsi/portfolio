About

I build identities that behave like systems — a mark, a grid, a type scale and the rules that keep them honest.

Seven years across studio and in-house work, mostly for food, culture and small manufacturers. I work from the constraints out: how it prints, how it stacks on a shelf, how it survives a junior designer six months from now. Every project ships with a specification, not a mood board.

Based in Manila. Working with clients across Southeast Asia and remote.
